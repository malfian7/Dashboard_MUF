Foundations

Radius & spacing

Corners get rounder as surfaces get bigger: 8–14 px for controls, 16–24 px for cards and bars, 28 px for hero surfaces, and a full pill for chips and nav actions.

Corner radius

Spacing scale gap / padding values in use

Foundations

Elevation

Shadows are tinted with the navy/ink colour rather than pure black. Coloured shadows (amber, green) mark highlighted surfaces such as the active tab and the Interest KPI.

Foundations

Motion

Quick and decisive: press feedback in under 200 ms, overlays around 250 ms, and a slow entrance for page content. All of it switches off under prefers-reduced-motion.
